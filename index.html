EyesOfPrep

Emergency supplies and prepper inventory app for Android

Know exactly what you've got before the next earthquake, storm or power cut. Snap a photo, let any AI chat read the label, and EyesOfPrep logs it for you.

Get it free on Google Play

Android only. Free to start. Pro is a one-time unlock: pay once, no subscription.

What it does

  • 📸 AI photo logging: snap an item in your favourite AI chat app, paste the answer back, and the name, category and details are filled in.
  • 🥫 Reserves: see how many days of food and water your household has, pets included.
  • ⏰ Expiry dates: a "use or replace first" list so nothing goes to waste.
  • 💧 Water & Filtration, 💊 Medical and 🌱 Seed Vault categories, with storage spots so you know where everything is.
  • 🎒 Bug-out bags: pack a bag for each person and check its weight against a safe carry limit.
  • ⛽ Fuel, energy and solar: bug-out range, power gear and a live solar forecast.
  • 🗺️ Offline maps with waypoints and a compass.

Private and offline

Everything you log stays on your phone. No account, no sign-in, no cloud and no ads. Logging, inventory, reserves and saved maps all work with no signal.
